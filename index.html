How this works

Editing content (left — Spines)

  • Click a theme's header to expand or collapse it.
  • Click into any title or description to edit — saves and syncs automatically.
  • Prep notes is for what needs to be ready beforehand.
  • Presenter assigns who's teaching it — click a name to assign, click again to unassign.
  • + Add lecture / 🗑 add or remove lectures; + Add theme creates a new theme with its own color.

Moving lectures (center — Calendar)

  • Drag a lecture to a new date, or click it once then click a date to place it there.
  • Dropping onto an occupied date swaps the two lectures.
  • Holiday and Friday-seminar slots reject invalid drops automatically — Fridays need a "standalone" lecture.
  • Under each date, the A / K / J circles mark Agnes/Kate/Jonathan absent that day.

Unscheduled lectures (right — Bank)

  • Drag a card here to pull it off the calendar.
  • Anything added without a date lands here automatically.

Good to know

  • Changes sync live for everyone within a second or two — no exporting or importing needed.
  • Export saves an occasional backup; not needed for day-to-day syncing.
  • Undo (⌘Z / Ctrl+Z, or the button) steps back through your own last ~20 changes.
