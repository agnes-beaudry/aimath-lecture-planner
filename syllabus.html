Course Plan

Four themes — each one ends in something you have actually built.

Theory of LMs

How language models work: tokens, context, transformers, training — and the difference between a model and the larger AI system built around it.

Project: Build a small language model in Python

AI-assisted research

Using AI tools to actually do mathematics: vibe-coding, vibe-proofing, agents, and harnesses.

Projects: Vibe-Coding Visualizations; AI-assisted research paper; Class-built harness

Formalization

An introduction to formalization languages — Lean — and to proof assistants and proof verification.

Project: Formalization of research paper; Add formalization harness

Ethical practices

The issues around AI in mathematical research: data sources, bias, resources, environmental cost, verification, responsibility, disclosure, reproducibility, attribution, consequences for mathematicians.

Project: Class written guidelines for responsible AI use in research

Grading

Class participation is the full grade. All students who participate will receive an A.
